# v1
You are classifying a healthcare / clinical-research organization from text scraped off its own
website. You are given one or more PAGE blocks (homepage and possibly a research/about page).
Classify the organization along TWO independent dimensions, plus a list of specialties.

Decide from what the SITE itself says. Do not use outside knowledge. If the pages are empty,
blocked, parked, or clearly not this organization, use business_type="other" and
research_involvement="unclear".

=========================================================================================
DIMENSION 1 — business_type  (pick EXACTLY ONE)
=========================================================================================
Answer the question: "If I landed on this site cold, what KIND of organization is this?"
— purely the form of the business, IGNORING whether it does research.

  hospital_health_system   — an inpatient hospital, or a system/network of hospitals and
                             affiliated facilities. INCLUDES academic medical centers and
                             university hospitals (don't break academic out separately).
  clinic_medical_practice  — outpatient CARE delivery: a physician practice, medical group,
                             or clinic — primary care, multispecialty, OR a single specialty
                             (oncology, fertility, pain, dermatology, etc.). Care is the product.
  clinical_research_site   — an organization whose OWN business is running clinical trials at
                             its site(s). Its identity is "we are a research site/center."
                             There is no general patient-care practice behind it.
  site_management_org      — an SMO / research-site NETWORK OPERATOR: the corporate or
                             management entity whose product is operating or managing a network
                             of research sites, often across many sponsors and locations.
                             Use this when the "we run/manage a network of sites" identity is
                             dominant. A single independent site is clinical_research_site.
  cro                      — Contract Research Organization: sells trial design / execution /
                             monitoring SERVICES to sponsors (pharma/biotech). Sponsor-side
                             vendor, not a care provider and not a site itself.
  biotech_pharma_device    — develops drugs, biologics, or medical devices; typically the
                             SPONSOR of trials, not a place patients go for care.
  government_community     — FQHC, community health center, VA / government health, or a
                             public-health department.
  nonprofit_foundation     — disease foundation, patient-advocacy group, or nonprofit research
                             consortium / institute. Also the natural home for an org that
                             COORDINATES or facilitates trials with hospitals/centers but does
                             not run a care practice or its own site.
  other                    — not a healthcare org at all (PE/holding company, staffing vendor,
                             marketing/agency site, parked or defunct domain), or impossible to tell.

DISAMBIGUATION:
  - clinic_medical_practice vs clinical_research_site: if there is a real CARE practice (you can
    book an appointment, see a doctor for treatment), it is clinic_medical_practice EVEN IF it
    also runs trials. clinical_research_site is for orgs whose whole reason to exist is trials.
  - clinical_research_site vs site_management_org: one site = clinical_research_site; an operator
    of a NETWORK of sites / a site-management company = site_management_org.
  - cro is sponsor-side services; do not confuse with site_management_org (site-side).

specialties  (list of strings; [] if none apply)
=========================================================================================
The medical / therapeutic specialties this org focuses on. Fill mainly for
clinic_medical_practice and hospital_health_system; for clinical_research_site list its
therapeutic focus areas if stated. Leave [] for CRO / biotech / SMO / nonprofit / other unless
a specialty is clearly central.

  - One specialty PER list item (e.g. ["Cardiology","Endocrinology"]), so each can be filtered

  - Use the CANONICAL name when a common one exists — always "Cardiology" (never "Heart Care"

    You MAY coin a new tag for an uncommon specialty not covered by a common name.
  - Use "Multispecialty" (as one tag) when the site describes itself as multispecialty without
    naming specific areas.

=========================================================================================
DIMENSION 2 — research_involvement  (pick EXACTLY ONE)
=========================================================================================
How central is clinical RESEARCH / TRIALS to this organization?

  primary    — clinical research/trials IS the core business. Remove the trials and there is
               little business left (dedicated sites, SMOs, most CROs by purpose).
  secondary  — the org runs REAL clinical trials, but alongside a different MAIN business
               (usually patient care). Remove the trials and it is still a functioning
               clinic/hospital.
  none       — no real clinical research/trials activity. Generic marketing language like
               "we research the latest treatments" or "research-backed care" does NOT count.
               Requires actual trials/studies/IRB/PI/coordinator signals to be anything but none.
  unclear    — the pages genuinely don't give enough signal to tell. Rare. NOT a hedge between
               two plausible answers — pick the better fit instead.

=========================================================================================
NEGATIVE EXAMPLES (these are the mistakes to avoid)
=========================================================================================
  - A fertility clinic (or pain / derm / cardiology practice) that ALSO runs trials →
    business_type=clinic_medical_practice, research_involvement=secondary.
    NOT clinical_research_site, NOT site_management_org. Its primary business is CARE.
  - A pain clinic with only marketing "research" language and no actual trials →
    business_type=clinic_medical_practice, research_involvement=none.
  - An org that coordinates/facilitates clinical research WITH hospitals & medical centers but
    runs no care practice and no site of its own → business_type=nonprofit_foundation
    (or clinical_research_site if it clearly markets itself AS a research organization),
    research_involvement=primary or secondary per how central research is.
  - A multi-location company that runs trials AND has lots of locations is NOT automatically
    site_management_org — only if it MANAGES/OPERATES sites as its product. Scale alone ≠ SMO.
